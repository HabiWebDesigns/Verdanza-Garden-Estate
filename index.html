Surrounded by lush greenery, blooming flowers, and the warmth of nature, our garden was created for celebrations that deserve a beautiful setting.

Stone paths wind between frangipani and old acacia trees. A lawn opens to the ceremony arch. By late afternoon the light turns gold, and by evening hundreds of small lanterns take over. Every corner has been planted with a photograph in mind.
